## 1. What we collect

| When you… | We collect | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Check a resume without an account | The text extracted from your PDF, the file name, and the analysis we generate | To analyse your resume and show you the result |
| Create an account (email or Google) | Name, email address, and a login identifier from Google if you use Google sign-in | To give you an account and save your work |
| Use the resume checker, builder, Resume Fix or Studio | Your resume text and the content you type or ask the AI to change | To provide the feature and save your versions |
| Do a mock or voice interview | The text of your answers (your browser turns speech into text; **we do not record or store audio**) and your scores and feedback | To run the interview and produce your report |
| Pay | Order details and payment status from Razorpay. **We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details** | To unlock what you paid for and keep billing records |
| Join through your college or institute | Name, email, roll number and batch provided by your institution, your consent record, and your interview results | So your institution can run placement practice and see your readiness |
| Take an institute's free readiness test | Name, mobile number, email, the course you're interested in, your consent, and your test result | To run the test and let that institute contact you about its course |
| Send an enquiry from our college page | Your name, role, institution, email, phone (optional) and message | To reply to your enquiry |

We also store a few settings in your browser (for example, whether you've seen a tip and your preferred currency) and a login session. We do **not** use advertising or analytics trackers.

For rate-limiting abuse on free tests and enquiries we keep a **one-way hash** of your IP address, phone and email — not the raw values.

## 2. How AI is used

Your resume text and interview answers are sent to our AI provider to generate analyses, rewrites, interview questions and feedback. AI output can be wrong: review it before you use it. Where our AI suggests a metric your resume doesn't contain, it leaves a placeholder like **[X%]** for you to fill in — it does not invent numbers for you.

We do not sell your data or use it to train our own AI models.

## 5. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us to: see the personal data we hold about you; correct or update it; delete it (subject to legal retention); withdraw consent (this stops future processing, not what was already done lawfully); and nominate someone to exercise your rights if you can't.

You can delete saved analyses from your dashboard at any time. For anything else, write to our Grievance Officer.

## 8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to each institution's data is restricted to that institution's staff. Payment details are handled only by Razorpay. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.
