HiResume's paid features are delivered instantly by AI, so most purchases can't be "returned". We still refund in the cases below.

## You'll get a full refund if
- **You were charged but didn't get what you paid for** — for example the payment went through but the Resume Fix, interview or pass wasn't unlocked, or generation failed and we couldn't fix it within [48 hours].
- **You were charged twice** for the same purchase.
- **We withdraw a feature** you've paid for access to — you get the unused portion back.

## Usually not refunded
- A Resume Fix, Resume Build or AI Interview that was successfully generated or completed.
- Unlimited Plan or Studio passes after you've used them, except where the law requires.

If you're unhappy with a result, write to us anyway — we'll try to put it right, for example by regenerating it.

## How to ask
Email **[support email — must be a working inbox]** within **[7 days]** of the payment, with the email on your account and the Razorpay payment ID or the date and amount. We'll reply within [2 working days].

## Timing
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method through Razorpay within **5–7 working days** (your bank may take longer to show it).

## Cancellations
Nothing auto-renews, so there's nothing to cancel. Passes and plans simply end at the end of their period.

## Institutions
Institution plans are governed by the agreement or invoice for that institution.
